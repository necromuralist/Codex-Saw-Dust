Information for the Fantagraphics Collection "The Million Year Picnic and Other Stories".

* Inside Story!
- Story: William M. Gaines, Al Feldstein
- Script: Al Feldstein
- Art: Will Elder
- [[https://eccomics.fandom.com/wiki/Weird_Science_Vol_1_14][EC Fandom Wiki]] - Weird Science Volume 1 Issue 14
- [[https://www.comics.org/issue/9835/#87850][GCD Listing]]
- July 1952

** Characters
- Professor Halpert Steinwell
- General Omarhower
- Smedley Throbbins (villain)
- Tony Bellano (villain)
- Charlie Grodsky (villain)
- Joe Erickton (villain) 
- Doctor Bowles
- unnamed coroner
- unnamed police lieutenant
- unnamed secretary of state

** Premise
Criminals begin dying by having their organs removed with no visible marks showing how they were taken. At the same time dying patients have their failing organs (which match the organs removed from the criminals) miraculously cured. The Coroner and the police have no idea what is going on.
